Rules I work by

More on X
  1. 01
    CTR and CPM are not KPIs.
    They don't move cost per conversion at the margin. I don't chase them daily.
  2. 02
    Never decide from a single day.
    One day of conversions is noise. Budget calls need three to seven days.
  3. 03
    A pixel winner is not a business winner.
    Scale on the platform metric, but let downstream quality veto it.
  4. 04
    Don't turn off a winner without a successor.
    The platform will retire a good ad early. Protect it by hand.
  5. 05
    Agents propose, people approve.
    Nothing changes a live budget on its own unless I delegated that exact job.
